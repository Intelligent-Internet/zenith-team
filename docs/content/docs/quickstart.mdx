Zenith Team runs your company through persistent [agents](/agents) that plan on your Zenith Team server and execute on your own computer. Getting to the first finished piece of work has six stops: get a server, create a [Company](/companies), connect a computer, meet your CEO Agent, approve a [Company Plan](/company-plan), and watch the first [run](/runs).

## 0. Get a server

Zenith Team is self-hosted. You need a Zenith Team server before you can sign in:

- **Run your own.** The [self-hosting quickstart](/self-hosting/quickstart) starts one on your machine with Docker in a few commands. It serves the app at `http://localhost:8080`.
- **Use your team's.** If someone already runs one, ask them for its address.

## 1. Sign up and create your Company

Open your server's address in a browser and create an account with your email and password. If the server has [email](/self-hosting/auth-setup#email-codes-and-password-reset) turned on, a code confirms the address first; if it has [Google sign-in](/self-hosting/auth-setup#google-sign-in), you can use that instead. Onboarding then asks a few questions about you and the company you are building and creates your [Company](/companies), the boundary every agent, plan, and conversation will live in.

The last onboarding screen sets up your [CEO Agent](/agents#the-ceo-agent-and-the-reporting-tree) — and it needs a connected computer, which you do not have yet.

**Success check:** the CEO Agent setup screen appears and **Registered runtimes** reports that no runtime is registered for this Company.

## 2. Connect your computer

Agents execute on your machine, not on the Zenith Team server — the [daemon](/daemon-and-runtimes) is the background process that makes that true, and it registers your machine's provider installations as runtimes. Two ways to get one running:

- **Desktop app** — it bundles the daemon and starts it for you once you are signed in. For now you [build it from source](/desktop-app) for your server.
- **CLI** — sign in to your server, then start the daemon in the background:

```bash
zen auth login --server https://zenith.example.com
zen daemon start
```

[Self-hosting quickstart](/self-hosting/quickstart#5-connect-a-computer-that-runs-agents) shows where to get `zen`. `zen auth login` prompts for email and password; `zen auth login --google` opens a browser sign-in instead. `zen daemon status` reports whether the daemon is up, and its state lives under `~/.zenith-team`.

Connecting a computer uploads nothing: your code, files, and workspace tree stay on the machine, and only conversations, statuses, and usage reach your Zenith Team server. The exact split is the [data and execution boundary](/how-zenith-team-works#the-data-and-execution-boundary).

<Callout type="info">
The daemon launches your local provider CLIs, so those must already be signed in on the same machine — Codex (`codex`) and Claude Code (`claude`) each with the vendor's own login. Zenith Team never holds provider credentials. See [Providers](/providers).
</Callout>

**Success check:** choose **Refresh** on the CEO Agent setup screen and your machine appears under **Registered runtimes** as online.

## 3. Meet your CEO Agent

On the setup screen, Zenith Team selects an online Codex runtime by default. You can choose another online runtime, optionally set a model, then save. If no Codex runtime is online, select a runtime explicitly or connect Codex. The four starter Agents inherit the CEO Agent's selected runtime and model settings. The [runtime](/daemon-and-runtimes) identifies the machine and provider installation. You then land in the app, in your CEO Agent's direct chat.

Now talk to your CEO Agent in its direct chat: what you want to build, who it is for, what success looks like. Zenith Team turns your input into a view of the business, introduces the [starter Agents](/agents#starter-agents), and plans the first Tasks your stage needs.

**Success check:** your CEO Agent has replied in its direct chat.

## 4. Approve your first Company Plan

The CEO Agent drafts a plan of numbered Tasks and dependencies. Applying a plan is consequential, so it raises an [approval](/approvals-and-questions): a frozen request for your verdict, waiting in **Approvals** (the `/approvals` page) and in the [Inbox](/notifications). Review the proposed tasks on **Work**, then approve.

How plan transactions and digests work is covered in [Company Plan](/company-plan) and [Approvals and questions](/approvals-and-questions).

**Success check:** **Work** (the `/tasks` page) shows your numbered Tasks.

## 5. Watch the first task run

Open **Work** and start a ready [task](/tasks). Starting it creates a run in the task's own conversation; a runtime on your computer claims it, and the provider CLI executes it in a [task worktree](/workspaces#task-worktrees) on your machine while the transcript streams back into the conversation.

A completed run is not a finished task: completion moves the task to `in_review`, and you decide when it is `done`. See [Run completion and task completion](/how-zenith-team-works#run-completion-and-task-completion).

**Success check:** the task's conversation holds the agent's transcript, and the task sits in `in_review`.

That closes the loop. What remains is reviewing this first output and coming back tomorrow — anything that needs you by then, from open approvals to questions, will be waiting in the [Inbox](/notifications).

## The surfaces you met

| Surface | Route | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | `/app` | The chat with your CEO Agent, your other sessions and the Company context sidebar |
| **Work** | `/tasks` | The [Company Plan](/company-plan) — your Tasks and dependencies |
| **Approvals** | `/approvals` | [Approvals](/approvals-and-questions) awaiting your verdict |
| **Inbox** | `/notifications` | The [Inbox](/notifications) — every unresolved item addressed to you |
| **Agents** | `/agents` | Your [Agents](/agents) and their reporting tree |

## If something does not move

| Symptom | Check first |
|---|---|
| **Registered runtimes** stays empty | Is the daemon running on that machine? Open the desktop app and sign in, or run `zen daemon status`. See [Daemon and runtimes](/daemon-and-runtimes). |
| A runtime is listed but offline | Bring that daemon back online — `zen daemon restart` — or select another runtime. |
| A run stays `queued` | No online runtime has claimed it. The run waits for its runtime, but fails with `queued_expired` if nobody claims it within two hours. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#a-run-stays-queued-or-fails-with-queued_expired). |
| Runs fail on an online runtime | Provider sign-in on that machine — the vendor CLI login for `codex` or `claude`, not anything in Zenith Team. See [Providers](/providers). |
| You cannot find the plan to approve | The [Inbox](/notifications) lists every unresolved item addressed to you. See [Approvals and questions](/approvals-and-questions). |
| A task finished but is not `done` | Completion means `in_review` — the review verdict is yours. See [Tasks](/tasks). |
