Zenith Team is a workspace where a founder operates persistent AI agents under a CEO Agent. Agents plan through tasks, execute with your local Codex and Claude Code CLIs, and keep you in the loop through approvals and notifications.

## What Zenith Team can do

- **Run a company of agents.** Persistent [agents](/agents) with their own identity, instructions and appearance, reporting to a CEO Agent.
- **Plan with Work.** Agents propose a [company plan](/company-plan); you approve it, and [tasks](/tasks) flow to the right agent.
- **Execute on your computer.** A [daemon](/daemon-and-runtimes) runs each task with your own [Codex or Claude Code](/providers) installation, inside a managed [workspace](/workspaces). Credentials never leave your machine.
- **Stay in the loop.** [Approvals and questions](/approvals-and-questions) bring decisions to you, and [notifications](/notifications) collect everything waiting on you.
