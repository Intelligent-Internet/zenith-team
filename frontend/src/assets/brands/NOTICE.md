# Brand assets

These are brand marks, separate from the Iconsax action and navigation icons.
They identify Zenith Team and the corresponding platforms; ownership remains with each
brand. Keep their geometry, proportions and original colors.

- `logo.svg`: Zenith Team wordmark with the orange Fold mascot. Its embedded
  PNG is an unchanged copy of `public/favicon/android-chrome-192x192.png`,
  preserving the existing transparent artwork and the 148 × 24 wordmark layout.
- `x.svg`: official [X brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit),
  [`x-logo.zip`](https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-logo.zip),
  `logo.svg`. Geometry and viewBox are unchanged; white fill becomes `currentColor`
  for the black mark on a light surface. Subject to X's brand guidelines.
- `instagram.svg`: Instagram's 2022 app logo from
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Instagram_logo_2022.svg),
  which attributes it to Instagram and labels it public domain as simple geometry.
  Editor metadata was removed; artwork and gradients are unchanged. Trademark
  rights remain with Instagram.
- `tiktok.png`: original 1200 × 1200 `TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png` from the
  [TikTok developer asset pack](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/getting-started-design-guidelines),
  [`logo-pack.zip`](https://sf16-va.tiktokcdn.com/obj/eden-va2/uvzhqeh7nuhd/tt4d/logo-pack.zip).
  The original PNG is bundled unchanged to retain the official multicolor mark.
  Subject to TikTok's brand guidelines.

Retrieved 2026-09-22. Facebook and LinkedIn reuse the existing bundled assets.

- `github.svg`: official [GitHub brand toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo),
  [logo archive](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip),
  `GitHub Logos/SVG/GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg`, retrieved 2026-10-01.
  Geometry, proportions and viewBox are unchanged; black fill becomes
  `currentColor` for legibility on light and dark surfaces. Used to identify
  the GitHub integration, subject to GitHub's brand guidelines.
