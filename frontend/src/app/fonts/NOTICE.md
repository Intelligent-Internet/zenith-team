# Workspace fonts

Self-hosted WOFF2 fonts from Fontsource 5.3.0 packages:

- [lato](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/lato/v/5.3.0), with license in `lato-LICENSE.txt`.
- [atkinson-hyperlegible-next](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-next/v/5.3.0), with license in `atkinson-hyperlegible-next-LICENSE.txt`.
- [noto-sans](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/noto-sans/v/5.3.0), with license in `noto-sans-LICENSE.txt`.
- [roboto-mono](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/roboto-mono/v/5.3.0), with license in `roboto-mono-LICENSE.txt`.
- [opendyslexic](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/opendyslexic/v/5.3.0), with license in `opendyslexic-LICENSE.txt`.
- [geist-mono](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/geist-mono/v/5.3.0) (variable weight), with license in `geist-mono-LICENSE.txt`; declared as "Geist Mono" in `geist-fonts.css`.
- [geist-pixel](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/geist-pixel/v/5.3.0), with license in `geist-pixel-LICENSE.txt`.

Includes available Latin, Latin Extended, and Vietnamese subsets at regular and bold weights, with italics where available. System fonts use locally installed fonts and platform fallbacks.
